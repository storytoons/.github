# StoryToons

Our mission is to accelerate child development and early learning
through technology and innovation.

We're early: exploring what that should look like, and building the
foundations while we figure it out. More at [storytoons.com](https://storytoons.com).

## For future collaborators

We care about doing this properly from day one: infrastructure as code,
trunk-based development, and no shortcuts on security, especially in a
product space involving children.

*Interested in early learning and technology? Reach out.*
